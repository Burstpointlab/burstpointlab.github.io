How it works

Every lane drops balls on a ladder of HP bars – the same ladder for every lane – but each lane follows its own math rule after every broken bar.

Before the countdown you tap the lane you think clears every bar first. The race is pure physics: nothing is nudged for or against you.

Call it right and your streak grows. Miss and it resets – that's the game.
